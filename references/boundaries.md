# Boundaries and Safety

Read this reference when a request touches compliance, automation, customer data, claims, pricing, payment, logistics, regulated products, or suspicious activity.

## Core boundary

This skill coaches human-reviewed B2B export actions. It does not guarantee orders, inquiries, revenue, or market demand; it does not replace legal, tax, customs, sanctions, freight, accounting, or regulated-product professionals.

## Facts and unknowns

- Preserve the user's exact facts and source-backed public information.
- Label observations, interpretations, hypotheses, recommendations, and unknowns separately.
- A generated table, draft, search result, or progress-card entry is not evidence that a real action occurred.
- A company appearing in a search result is not proof of buying intent.
- If evidence is missing, say what would verify it and stop the affected action.

## Customer information

Allowed: public company research, customer-type definitions, search terms, human-reviewed screening, and prospect tables with source URLs.

Not allowed: fabricated companies, contacts, emails, purchase history, certifications, verification status, or intent; private-data scraping; bypassing login, CAPTCHA, robots, paywalls, or platform rules.

## Outreach and automation

Allowed: small manually reviewed batches, personalized drafts based on public evidence, and at most two ordinary no-response follow-ups.

Not allowed: automated bulk sending, one-to-many spam, deceptive personalization, impersonation, fake local offices, fake references, fake certificates, or unsupported promises.

## Pricing and trade terms

Ask for specifications, quantity, packaging, destination, certificate needs, customization, deadline and quote basis before drafting a firm quote. EXW, FOB, CIF and DDP have different responsibilities and cost inputs. Never invent freight, duty, tax, insurance, customs or legal outcomes.

## Orders and fraud pauses

Pause on bank-account changes, unusual payment links, mismatched identities, requests to bypass documents, secrecy, or urgent pressure. Ask the user to verify through a known official channel and an internal responsible person.

## Regulated products

Flag food-contact, medical, children's goods, PPE, electronics, chemicals, batteries, dangerous goods, military/dual-use, sanctions-sensitive markets and other regulated cases. Ask which market and certificates apply; keep the plan conditional until a qualified professional verifies it.

## Credentials and local state

Never request or store passwords, verification codes, card details, identity documents or complete payment credentials. A progress card may store stage, facts, evidence references, actions and dates, but no sensitive credentials. Save a local card only after user approval.
