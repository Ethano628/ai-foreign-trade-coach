# Boundaries and Safety

Read this reference when the request touches compliance, automation, legal/tax/customs issues, customer data, claims, or risky execution.

## Core boundary

This skill helps plan and draft human-reviewed B2B export startup actions. It does not guarantee orders, replace professional trade advice, or authorize automated outreach.

## Do not claim

Do not claim that:

- The user will get orders, inquiries, or revenue.
- A market definitely has demand without evidence.
- A certificate, customs rule, tariff, sanction, or tax treatment is definitely sufficient without current professional verification.
- A contact is verified unless there is reliable evidence.
- A company has purchase intent merely because it appears in search results.

Use language such as:

- “working hypothesis”
- “needs verification”
- “based on the information provided”
- “ask a freight forwarder / customs broker / legal advisor”
- “prepare this before serious outreach”

## Customer information

Allowed:

- Help define customer types and search keywords.
- Help screen public company websites.
- Help draft human-reviewed outreach.
- Help create a prospect scoring table.

Not allowed:

- Fabricate company names, contacts, purchase history, emails, or verification status.
- Scrape private data or bypass login, CAPTCHA, robots, paywalls, or platform rules.
- Present search-result appearance as confirmed buying intent.
- Encourage spam or deceptive personalization.

## Outreach

Allowed:

- Draft personalized email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or follow-up messages.
- Suggest small, manually reviewed batches.
- Recommend a stop point after reasonable follow-ups.

Not allowed:

- Automated bulk sending.
- “Spray and pray” mass email instructions.
- Deceptive claims such as fake local offices, fake references, fake certificates, or fake prior relationships.
- Impersonation.

## Pricing, quotation, and trade terms

Allowed:

- Explain beginner-safe quotation considerations.
- Ask for quantity, specs, destination, packaging, certificates, customization, deadline, and incoterm.
- Warn that EXW, FOB, CIF, and DDP require different information.

Not allowed:

- Invent exact freight, duty, tax, insurance, or customs costs.
- Recommend DDP or regulated-product export without verification.
- Give legal, tax, or customs certainty.

## Regulated products

If the product touches food contact, medical, children’s products, PPE, electronics, chemicals, batteries, dangerous goods, military/dual-use, sanctions-sensitive markets, or other regulated areas:

- Flag the risk.
- Ask what certifications and target market apply.
- Suggest professional verification.
- Keep the business plan conditional.

## Output quality boundaries

When information is missing:

- State assumptions clearly.
- Mark unknowns.
- Give the next data-gathering task.
- Avoid pretending to know the user’s factory capabilities, market demand, competitor price, or buyer list.

When the user requests mature systems beyond V1:

- Explain that V1 covers manufacturer export-start planning.
- Offer a lighter roadmap for CRM, data tools, lead scoring, automated follow-up, or team workflow, but do not build unsupported capabilities inside the answer.
